# Phase 7.1 Plan 08: Company expenses against the Phase 6 rules Summary

**Every legacy company expense is turned into the add-expense request (Others category, payer through people matching) and judged by the Phase 6 field validator and the handler's category and payer checks, with every failure listed at once and nothing saved or audited.**

## Accomplishments

- Tracer (D-12): a made-up company expense with amount 0 and payer text `SENTINEL-NOBODY` ends a full run with exit 0, the console line `Company expenses checked: 1.`, and findings for `Amount` (`ExpenseRules.AmountTooSmall`), `PaidByUserId` (`ExpenseRules.PaidByInactive`), CannotMap `company-expense.no-category` and LegacyHolderUsed. Through the `BeforeTrialDropped` hook the trial database holds 0 company expenses and the same audit row count as right after it was created. The tracer `<verify>` was re-run before expanding: `FitCheckRunnerTests` 12 of 12.
- `DraftMapping.MapCompanyExpense`: date is the Manila reading as `yyyy-MM-dd` (an empty date stays null so the validator reports it), category is Others, description trimmed, amount as stored, payer from `PeopleMatching.Resolve` with the holder findings used for orders, notes empty. A differing date reading is an Info `dates.reading-differs` finding, as for orders.
- `CompanyExpenseCheck.CheckAsync`: runs `CreateCompanyExpenseRequestValidator` with the business today under `TodayKey`, then the category lookup (unknown gives `CategoryRequired`, retired gives `CategoryRetired`) and the payer lookup (`PaidByInactive` unless the person can pay), in the handler's order and only when the ids are set. One ExpenseRule finding per failure. It never calls the handler (`grep -rc CreateCompanyExpenseHandler tools/Csps.LegacyFitCheck` is 0 for every file) and saves nothing.
- `TrialImport.RunAsync` takes an optional list of mapped company expenses and checks them in one scope of the trial provider after the order imports and the counter findings. `FitCheckRunner` reads the rows with `isCompanyExpenses` set, adds the mapping findings, and prints `Company expenses checked: {n}.` after the orders line.
- `CompanyExpenseCheckTests`: 8 cases on a migrated `CSPS_Test_{guid}` database: three field failures in one call (amount 0, date 31 days ahead, description over 200), unknown category, retired category, active seeded staff passes, Legacy holder cannot pay, unknown payer cannot pay, empty ids only judged by the validator (no lookups), missing date. Every case also asserts 0 company expenses and an unchanged audit count.
- `07.1-DRAFT-MAPPING.md` has a new "Company expenses" section and the order-parts row now points to it. Only the Updated timestamp changed.

## Notes and open questions for the owner and later plans

- **Open question (from plan 04, still open):** a legacy expense with `isCompanyExpenses` false and no `OrderId` is read into no order and is not read by this check either. The tool does not report such rows. The owner can rule whether the fit check should count them (a new finding), or whether Phase 15 handles them. Not decided here.
- **Plan 09:** count ExpenseRule findings by rule key (`{field}: {message}`); the Legacy-holder payer case appears as `PaidByUserId: ...` plus the holder findings on the same expense. Company expenses and order expenses use separate rule keys (`company-expense.no-category` and `order-expense.no-category`).
- A legacy company expense payer who matches an Admin legacy user maps to a pseudonymous trial staff member that can pay, so only unmatched, blank or non-Admin payers raise `PaidByUserId`.
- The date rule uses the Manila reading (provisional, 07.1 A3); a company expense dated late in a UTC day may differ by one day under the other reading, counted by `dates.reading-differs`.
