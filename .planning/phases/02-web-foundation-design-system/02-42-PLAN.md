## Gap Being Closed: G-2-199, truth 28 (02-VERIFICATION.md frontmatter `gaps:`, status partial)

**Truth:** "Pressing the backdrop's own scrollbar scrolls it and never closes the dialog (no lost type-to-confirm text); a press on the scrim outside the scrollbar still closes it, as before."

**Why it is partial (verifier probe, real Chromium):** `pressedOnScrollbar` (`Modal.tsx:59-68`) recognises only scrollbars that take layout space. With overlay scrollbars (`--enable-features=OverlayScrollbar`) a real drag at clientX 1276 < clientWidth 1280 scrolled the backdrop by 105px, fired mousedown on the region, the guard said "not a scrollbar" and the dialog closed with ORD-2026-0148 lost. `onMouseDown` (`:113-117`) also ignores `event.button`, so a middle press (Windows autoscroll) or a right press closes it (WR-01). No E2E test ever presses a real scrollbar, because headless Chromium starts with `--hide-scrollbars` (WR-02).

**Owner decision (LOCKED, D-42 in 02-CONTEXT.md):** "Pressing the backdrop's scrollbar never closes the dialog, so typed confirm text is never lost." Dialogs stay centred; the 140px (96px) gap shrinks to 16px (0); the backdrop scrolls when still too tall. The layout part is done and verified (truths 24-27, 29, 30); this plan changes only the close rule and its proof.

**Design (chosen under Claude's discretion, no new dependency, no markup change):** the scrim closes on a **click**, not on the mousedown, and only when:
1. the latest mousedown was the **primary button** and its target was a scrim, not on a classic scrollbar (`pressedOnScrollbar`, kept);
2. the click is the primary button and its target is a scrim;
3. every scrim's `scrollTop` and `scrollLeft` are the same as at that press (a drag scrolled the backdrop, so it was a scroll, not a press on the dark area). Focus moving during the press can scroll the backdrop too (Firefox can focus a scrollable element on a press, and the focus trap then pulls focus back into the dialog), so the positions are re-read on every `focusin` while the press is pending.

Why this holds for overlay scrollbars: a drag changes the scroll position (rule 3, engine-independent), and Chromium and WebKit send no `click` after a press that the scrollbar handled (rules 1-2). It also fixes middle and right presses (rule 1 and 2; browsers send `auxclick`, not `click`, for them). It matches the project's MobileNavDrawer, which already closes "on a click on the scrim (a click, not a mousedown …)". The verifier's other option, a dedicated backdrop layer, adds an element (a D-08 markup change with a parity structural exception), so it is held back as option (b) of the "Overlay scrollbar press without a drag" checkpoint.
