**The public site now shows no cookie bar, no Cookie Settings button and no Clear dialog entry point; the Contact phone bar is always on; the dev previews, showcase demos and consent e2e journeys are deleted, and a new `@no-cookies` spec proves an empty cookie jar in all five browser projects.**
