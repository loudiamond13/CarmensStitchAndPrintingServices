# Phase 12.1 Plan 14: Visitor Stats reads only the daily page counts and answers page views, the top 5 pages and the trend

**The Visitor Stats answer now holds just the page view total, the five most viewed pages with shares, and a per day or per month trend, all summed from DailyPageCounts; the old totals, sources, devices, browsers, speed and cut date, the speed rating helpers and the visit seeding are gone, and all 40 `@visitor-stats` e2e runs pass.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 135 min (the whole-project Server run took 19 min of it)
- **Tasks:** 3 (Task 1 tracer, Tasks 2 and 3 auto), all TDD
- **Commits:** 6 (3 RED, 2 feat, 1 refactor)
- **Files changed:** 28 in the tree (474 insertions, 2,118 deletions; 2 deleted, 2 renamed, none created)

## Accomplishments

- `VisitorStatsResponse(Range, From, To, PageViews, Bucket, Trend, TopPages)` and `TrendBucketDto(Start, PageViews)`. The totals, speed, vital and cut date types are deleted.
- `IVisitorStatsReader` has `ReadPageViewsAsync(first, last)`, `ReadTrendAsync(first, last, bucket)` and `ReadPagesAsync(first, last)`, all EF LINQ over `DailyPageCounts` (sums as `long`, capped to `int.MaxValue`). `ReadDailyAsync` and `DailyCount` stay for the CSV export.
- `GetVisitorStatsHandler`: `ListLength = 5`, shares from `Shares.LargestRemainder` over the full ordered list and then the first 5; `Fill` has no cut logic; `Resolve` is unchanged for the export.
- OpenAPI snapshot and `api.generated.ts` regenerated in the same commit as the contract change. `parseVisitorStats` reads top-level `pageViews` and the new trend buckets and still produces the same `VisitorStats`.
- The Testing seed takes `{ offsetDays, pageKey, views }`, deletes and writes `DailyPageCounts` only, keeps the 3-year window and `IsDatabaseAllowed`, and answers 400 with a plain text of the new rules.
- Deleted `VitalRating`, `VitalGrade`, `NearestRank` and `ReferrerName.From`. `VitalBounds` moved to its own file; the five name constants and `MaxLength` stay.
- The `@visitor-stats` e2e journeys now match the page plan 12.1-08 built and the answer from this plan.
