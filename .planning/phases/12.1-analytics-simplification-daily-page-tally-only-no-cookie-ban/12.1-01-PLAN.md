The client half of D-53 and the speed part of D-59: the beacon stops waiting for the cookie bar and sends `{ path }` for every new path, and the web-vitals speed reader is removed and uninstalled (D-59 reverses D-38).

Purpose: after this plan the browser sends nothing but the page path, which is what D-53 keeps.
Output: path-only beacon, no speed reader, web-vitals uninstalled and recorded, a new source-scan gate.
</objective>
