# Phase 12.1 Plan 08: A Manager sees one Page views figure, a bars-only chart and the most viewed pages, in plain words

**Visitor Stats now parses and shows only the page view total, a bars-only per day or per month chart and the most viewed pages; the visitors, visits, sources, devices and page speed cards, their previews, copy, recipes and the circle-x icon are deleted, and the page reads in plain page-view words.**

## Accomplishments

- `VisitorStats` is range, from, to, pageViews, bucket, trend (`{ start, pageViews }`) and topPages. `VisitorTotals`, the speed types and every parser for the cut date, sources, devices, browsers and speed are gone. The parser still reads today's answer (`totals.pageViews`); plan 12.1-14 changes only its input.
- `VisitorTrendChart` has no unique visitor line, points, swatch, tooltip row or axis input. The tooltip is the date and "Page views {n}"; the announcement reads the same text.
- `VisitorKpiRow` takes `pageViews` and renders one `KpiCard`. `VisitorStatsPage` dropped the cut note, the sources list and the device and speed cards.
- Deleted `PageSpeedCard` (+ test) and `DeviceBrowserCard`; `circle-x` is out of `icons.ts` and `Icon.test.tsx`.
- Copy (all `// SPEC-GAP`): subline "{dates} · Page views on the public website. Staff signed in to admin aren't counted."; KPI "Page views" with hint "Every page opened on the public website"; trend "Page views over time" (Per day / Per month); chart group "Page views, {from} to {to}"; empty state "No page views in these dates" / "Page views show here after people open the website. - Removed the copy and recipe entries only the deleted parts used (`cutNote`, visitor KPI copy, `speed`, `devices`, sources and device list copy, `chartVisitor*`, `deviceBrowser*`, `pageSpeed*` classes).
