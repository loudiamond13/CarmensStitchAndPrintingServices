This closes the advisory I listed last time (IN-G1-03, a rename could turn the ceiling off). The change is a refactor plus a test. It adds no behavior.

(`SiteBusyDetail`, chosen in `WriteRejectionAsync` by `SiteWideCeilingLimiter.Refused`). A per-address refusal keeps its own text, and the per-address-first chain order is unchanged. `AnalyticsRateLimitTests` asserts the busy text (line 142) and passes.
