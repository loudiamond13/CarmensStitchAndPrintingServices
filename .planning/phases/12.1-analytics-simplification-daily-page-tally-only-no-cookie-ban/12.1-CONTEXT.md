# Phase 12.1: Analytics simplification: daily page tally only, no cookie banner - Context

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

The public site writes only the anonymous daily page tally (D-53), sets no optional cookies and shows no cookie banner (D-54). The Contact map loads after a tap, and the tap is saved in the browser so the map loads by itself next time (D-65, which amends D-55). Visitor Stats shows total page views, the top 5 pages and a per day or per month chart, with a CSV export (D-57, D-67 to D-70). The Cookie Policy is folded into the Privacy Policy (D-72), and the owner approves the new Privacy Policy wording at a checkpoint (D-73). The unused tables stay until a separate drop approval (D-56, D-61, D-64).

Carried forward from Phase 12, unchanged: bots and signed-in staff are not counted (D-21); the site-wide ceiling on the anonymous routes that remain (D-51); daily totals are kept about 3 years and removed at the monthly cleanup (D-04, D-46); D-50 no longer applies (no referrers stored). With no cookie bar, D-30 (hide the Messenger and Call bar until the cookie bar is answered) no longer applies: the bar always shows on phones.

Not in scope: dropping any table or column (needs its own owner yes, D-56); Terms of Use and Accessibility Statement wording (only their links to the Cookie Policy change, if any).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

All rulings below: owner, 2026-11-18, in this discussion. Numbering continues from Phase 12 (D-01 to D-58 are in `.planning/phases/12-consent-analytics-legal/12-CONTEXT.md`).

### Leftover cookie code
- **D-59:** **Remove all the old consent code.** The cookie bar, the Cookie Settings window, the footer "Cookie settings" button, the consent route (`POST /api/v1/analytics/consent`), the clear route (`POST /api/v1/analytics/clear`), the speed sample route (`POST /api/v1/analytics/vitals`), their handlers, validators, ports and tests, the client consent modules (`consentCookie`, `consentJar`, `consentPolicy`, `consentRules`, `consentFocus`, `consentCopy`) and the dev previews and scenarios for them. The `web-vitals` package (D-38) is uninstalled. Only the page view route stays, and it only increments `DailyPageCounts`. The database tables stay (D-56). — **Reversibility:** costly — bringing stats back means rebuilding the consent flow, routes and tests.
- **D-60:** **Keep `/.well-known/gpc.json`** (D-39). It stays true: the site tracks no one, so it honors the signal by default.
- **D-61:** **The consent record table stays, unused, like D-56.** Nothing new is written. The monthly cleanup keeps removing old rows. Dropping it joins the D-56 drop list and needs a separate owner yes.
- **D-62:** **Phase 12 and Phase 12.1 go live together.** Phase 12 is never deployed alone, so no real visitor ever sees the cookie bar.
- **D-63:** **The server expires the old consent cookie and the old visitor id cookie when a browser still sends them** (browsers used in testing). Then the site can truthfully say it sets no optional cookies.
- **D-64:** **The monthly roll-up into the daily device and source totals stops.** Those two tables (`DailyDeviceCount`, `DailyReferrerCount`) stay, unused, and join the D-56 drop list. The monthly cleanup still ages out old rows in every analytics table.

### Map tap
- **D-65:** **The Show Map tap is saved in the browser's local storage (amends D-55).** After one tap, the map loads by itself on every later visit from that browser. There is still no cookie banner: the tap itself is the choice.
  - The saved setting has no expiry. It lasts until the visitor clears their browser data.
  - There is no on-page button to undo it. The Privacy Policy explains how to clear it in the browser.
  - It is saved for browsers that send GPC too: the tap is a clear choice by the visitor (same reasoning as D-20).
  - One setting covers every Google map on the site, now and later.
  - The Privacy Policy lists this one saved setting (D-72).
- **D-66:** **Map wording and Directions stay as they are.** The box keeps "The map loads from Google when you tap Show Map." with no extra line about Google cookies. The Directions link stays a plain link that needs no tap first.

### Visitor Stats details
- **D-67:** **The most viewed pages list shows the top 5**, each with its count and share of all views. The full detail is in the CSV.
- **D-68:** **The chart keeps choosing per day or per month by itself** (per day up to 62 days, per month for longer ranges, `StatsBucket`). No Day / Month switch.
- **D-69:** **The CSV export is one row per day per page:** `Date,Page,Page views`. Days with no views for a page are left out. This replaces the D-25 / D-33 export shape. The file name keeps the `visitor-stats-{from}-to-{to}.csv` pattern.
- **D-70:** **The date picker reaches back 3 years**, matching what is kept (D-04). The 13-month note, the "Not available" markers and the unique visitors and visits figures are removed (D-27 no longer applies).

### Policy wording and version
- **D-71:** **The Privacy Policy stays version 1.0, rewritten in place, with the DRAFT notice back on until the owner re-approves it.** No visitor ever saw the first 1.0, because the site is not live (D-62).
- **D-72:** **The Cookie Policy is folded into the Privacy Policy (amends D-58).** The Privacy Policy gets a section on cookies and saved settings: the necessary cookies, the saved map setting (D-65), that Google may set its own cookies when the map loads, and how to clear them. The `/cookies` page is removed and shows the normal "page not found" (no redirect). The footer Legal row has three links: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Accessibility Statement. Links to the Cookie Policy from other pages move to the Privacy Policy. — **Reversibility:** reversible — a new page and footer link can be added back in a later release.
- **D-73:** **Review flow: Claude drafts the new Privacy Policy wording in plain words, then a plan stops at a checkpoint for the owner's yes**, like Phase 12's plan 12-22. If the owner is away, the checkpoint is parked in the backlog and the page keeps its DRAFT notice.

### Planning rulings (owner, 2026-11-18, during /gsd-plan-phase 12.1)
- **D-74:** **Build the D-51 site-wide ceiling in this phase.** Research found it was never built (only per-address limits exist). Add one global limit on the page view route only, about 600 calls a minute, as the setting `RateLimits:AnalyticsCeiling`, validated at start-up. Test hosts raise it so suites are not throttled.
- **D-75:** **The CSV Page column shows the page name** (for example `Home`), not the page key. Server and web tests read the names from one shared fixture.
- **D-76:** **Remove the `circle-x` icon** (approved in D-32 only for the "Poor" speed rating), since page speed is removed.
- **D-77:** **The cleanup report line becomes "Old daily page counts removed".**
- **D-78:** **Workflow:** Phase 12.1 reuses `12-UI-SPEC.md` as its design contract (no 12.1 UI-SPEC); new copy stays `// SPEC-GAP`. Research ran first. [informational]

### Claude's Discretion
- How the server expires the old cookies (D-63): which response, which routes, and the tests.
- How the Contact page reads the saved map setting without a flash of the placeholder, and the local storage key name.
- The Visitor Stats copy updates (subline, KPI labels, empty state) once unique visitors and visits are gone. New copy is `// SPEC-GAP` and follows OR-4 (no long dash).
- How the public bundle budget (D-42) is kept: removing the consent bar and web-vitals should free room.
- Updating the `LegalPages` list (server and client), the sitemap and any e2e tests (including `web/e2e/gpc.spec.ts`) for the removed page and routes.
- The Privacy Policy rewrite also drops lines that no longer apply: visit and session detail, referrers, devices, page speed, consent record retention (D-05) and "Clear my data" (D-19, D-44). The "Who else handles your data" list (D-40) stays, with Google only when the map is loaded.

</decisions>
