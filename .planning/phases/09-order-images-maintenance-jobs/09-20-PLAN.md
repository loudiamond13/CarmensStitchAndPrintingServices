<assumption_delta_decision>
Primary noun: `OrderImage.Position` (0 is the cover).
Decision: no-change. The cover stays a derived value, the photo at Position 0, exactly as CONTEXT D-14 and the UI-SPEC data model ("position 1 is the cover"; "Make Cover moves that photo to position 1 and shifts the others right") rule; "Make Cover" is a move to position 0, so no stored "chosen cover" field is added and the identity model (Position orders the photos, Slot only guards the 5-photo limit) still names the right thing.
</assumption_delta_decision>

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff can attach, arrange and view order photos, including straight from a phone camera. The photos load fast and stay private per the OD-9 ruling. Storage cleanup and token purges run as short, reliable, scheduled jobs."
</objective>
