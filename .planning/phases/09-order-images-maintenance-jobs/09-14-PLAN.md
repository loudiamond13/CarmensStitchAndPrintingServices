# Plan 09-14: Full-size phone photos upload, and a retry never stores a photo twice

*Created: 2026-10-31T18:00:00-05:00 · Updated: 2026-10-31T11:52:00-05:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 9 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
Raise the body limit for the photo upload route only (10 MB file plus form overhead) through every layer that caps it today (the body-limit middleware, Kestrel, IIS in-process and web.config), keep every buffer in memory, hash multipart uploads part by part so "Try Again" with the same key replays instead of failing, and protect the 256 MB app pool with a per-address upload limit and a two-at-a-time concurrency cap.

Purpose: IMG-02 (max 10 MB, never on local disk) and SEC-06 for the upload POST (RESEARCH Pattern 7, Pitfalls 1 to 3; LIB-CR-02 closed).
Output: moved and per-endpoint body limit, idempotency changes, web.config, rate limits, tests and docs.

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff can attach, arrange and view order photos, including straight from a phone camera. The photos load fast and stay private per the OD-9 ruling. Storage cleanup and token purges run as short, reliable, scheduled jobs."
</objective>
