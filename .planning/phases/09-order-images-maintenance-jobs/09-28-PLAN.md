# Plan 09-28: The monthly run removes deleted photos from Cloudinary and sweeps stray files

*Created: 2026-10-31T18:00:00-05:00 · Updated: 2026-10-31T11:52:00-05:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 9 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
Two maintenance jobs on top of the queue (09-24) and the adapter (09-19): the removal job works the queue with per-row leases, retries with growing waits and a red run when removals keep failing; the stray sweep finds files in the environment's own orders folder that no photo row knows, older than 2 days, and queues them.

Purpose: JOB-02 "retried until they succeed ... failures are logged and an orphan sweep reconciles storage", D-05, D-21 (monthly, all in one run), D-22, D-23.
Output: the claim API on the queue, the inventory reader, both jobs, their tests and the runbook update.

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff can attach, arrange and view order photos, including straight from a phone camera. The photos load fast and stay private per the OD-9 ruling. Storage cleanup and token purges run as short, reliable, scheduled jobs."
</objective>
