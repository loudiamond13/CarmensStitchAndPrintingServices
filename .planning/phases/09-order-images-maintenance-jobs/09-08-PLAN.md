# Plan 09-08: The photo list: add photos from a computer or a phone, and see each photo's controls

*Created: 2026-10-31T18:00:00-05:00 · Updated: 2026-10-31T11:52:00-05:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 9 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
Rebuild the shared `ImageUploader` into the presentational photo manager the UI-SPEC describes: a drop zone on computers and "Take Photo" and "Choose Photos" on phones, a compact list of `PhotoTile`s with always-visible 44 px controls, five tile states and three modes. It sends every action to callbacks; the order screens (09-16, 09-21, 09-23, 09-27, 09-29) wire them.

Purpose: ORD-04 accessible reorder and cover (D-13, D-14, WCAG 2.5.7), IMG-05 phone capture entry (D-17), and the D-25 compact list.
Output: rebuilt ImageUploader, new PhotoTile, recipe and copy, the class-gate entry for the grip, the rewritten dev scenarios and the updated D-05 e2e check.

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff can attach, arrange and view order photos, including straight from a phone camera. The photos load fast and stay private per the OD-9 ruling. Storage cleanup and token purges run as short, reliable, scheduled jobs."
</objective>
