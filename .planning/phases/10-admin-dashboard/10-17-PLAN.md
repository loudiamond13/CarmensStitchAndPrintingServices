# Plan 10-17: Staff read their ledger on My Money, page by page, with the balance after each entry

*Created: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 10 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
The ledger in the browser (UI-SPEC M1 "Ledger card", D-41, MONEY-02): every row of the caller's money with its details, Money in or Money out, and the server's running balance, server-paged with the page in the URL (WEB-10), plus the Manager-only Show deleted view (D-30, D-31). The card is built once for M1 here and reused for one person's page in plan 10-19; the row actions arrive in plan 10-20.

Purpose: MONEY-02 complete: a staff member sees their own money on hand and the ledger behind it.
Output: the table, the card, the URL hook, the query, the service call, the page wiring, copy, fixtures and an e2e Back check.

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff land on a dashboard that shows how the business stands today: active work, money owed, money collected and money spent. Each staff member also sees the money they hold and can hand it to a coworker (owner addition, 2026-10-07)."
</objective>
