# Plan 10-05: Staff get the dashboard summary from one API call

*Created: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 10 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
The dashboard's server side for everything except the chart (RESEARCH Pattern 5): one `GET /api/v1/dashboard/summary` returns the business today, the greeting part, the caller's first name, the four KPI figures with their foot data (C-40: D-01 to D-07, D-11, D-54, D-55), the six status counts (D-08) and the 6 newest orders (D-38). One request gives one consistent snapshot at a week or month boundary. Plan 10-07 renders it.

Output: the handler, port, reader, controller, registrations, three test classes, the matrix row, the OpenAPI snapshot and web types, and `docs/api/dashboard.md`.

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff land on a dashboard that shows how the business stands today: active work, money owed, money collected and money spent. Each staff member also sees the money they hold and can hand it to a coworker (owner addition, 2026-10-07)."
