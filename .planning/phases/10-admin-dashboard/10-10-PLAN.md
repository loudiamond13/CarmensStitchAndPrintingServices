# Plan 10-10: A staff member transfers money to a coworker, and two transfers can never overdraw

*Created: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 10 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
The transfer write (RESEARCH Pattern 4): `POST /api/v1/money-transfers` records a transfer from the signed-in user to a coworker under the `staff-money` lock (MONEY-04, MONEY-05, D-14, D-19, D-20, D-49, D-53, D-56), idempotent per SEC-06 and stamped per SEC-08. The recipient rule follows D-56, not the payer rule: Manager-locked and not-yet-set-up active staff can receive, so `StaffState.CanPay` is not reused (pattern-mapper note).

D-53 ruling (owner, 2026-11-05) recorded for this plan: only transfer create and hand-over take the `staff-money` lock; expense, payment and refund saves do not, because they may already take a balance below zero (D-15, D-16). The race test below backs it.

Purpose: success criterion 6 for the normal transfer: above zero, not to self, not future-dated, never more than held, one row per double-click.
Output: request, validator, handler, response, recipient rule, lock port and adapter, store, controller action, tests, matrix row, contract and docs.

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff land on a dashboard that shows how the business stands today: active work, money owed, money collected and money spent. Each staff member also sees the money they hold and can hand it to a coworker (owner addition, 2026-10-07)."

Serial contract rule: the plans that rewrite `tools/openapi-gen/openapi.v1.json`, `web/src/types/api.generated.ts` and `AuthorizationMatrix.cs` are 10-05, 10-06, 10-08, 10-10, 10-12, 10-14, 10-16 and 10-18, each in its own wave.
</objective>
