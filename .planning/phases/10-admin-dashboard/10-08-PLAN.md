# Plan 10-08: Each staff member reads their own money on hand from the server

*Created: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 10 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
The money tracer on the server (RESEARCH Patterns 1, 2 and 4): `GET /api/v1/staff-money/me` returns the caller's money on hand from six sources (MONEY-01). Because transfers are one of the sources, this plan also brings the `MoneyTransfers` table (the one Phase 10 migration, RESEARCH Pitfall 13) and the `staff-money` standing lock row that plan 10-10 takes. Plans 10-10 and 10-12 add the writes, 10-14 the ledger, 10-16 everyone's balances.

Purpose: a staff member's balance is always server-computed from the records, never stored (Core Value).
Output: the EF configuration and migration, the lock name, the reader, port, handler, controller, registrations, tests, matrix row, contract and `docs/api/staff-money.md`.

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff land on a dashboard that shows how the business stands today: active work, money owed, money collected and money spent. Each staff member also sees the money they hold and can hand it to a coworker (owner addition, 2026-10-07)."

Serial contract rule: the plans that rewrite `tools/openapi-gen/openapi.v1.json`, `web/src/types/api.generated.ts` and `AuthorizationMatrix.cs` are 10-05, 10-06, 10-08, 10-10, 10-12, 10-14, 10-16 and 10-18, each in its own wave. This is the only Phase 10 plan that adds a migration.

Reversibility: D-23 (soft delete with Deleted by and Deleted at) is rated one-way in CONTEXT and was ruled by the owner on 2026-11-05, so it carries the rating without a new checkpoint (the door is already chosen). `IsHandover` is this plan's own choice (assumption-delta decision in plan 10-03), rated costly; owner-confirmed as D-57 (2026-11-05).
</objective>
