# Plan 10-14: Staff read their own ledger with a running balance after every row

*Created: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-06T23:12:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 10 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
The ledger read (RESEARCH Pattern 3, MONEY-02, D-41): `GET /api/v1/staff-money/me/ledger` returns one page of the caller's money rows from all six sources, newest first, each with its details and the running balance after it. It builds on the union proved in plan 10-01 by adding transfers in and out (the table from plan 10-08), with the Manager-only deleted view (D-30, D-31). Plan 10-16 adds the same ledger for another person; plan 10-17 draws it.

Purpose: a staff member can see exactly how their money on hand came to be, row by row.
Output: the union extension, the reader page and detail reads, the request, validator, handler, pure running-balance helper, response, controller action, tests, matrix row, contract and docs.

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff land on a dashboard that shows how the business stands today: active work, money owed, money collected and money spent. Each staff member also sees the money they hold and can hand it to a coworker (owner addition, 2026-10-07)."

Serial contract rule: the plans that rewrite `tools/openapi-gen/openapi.v1.json`, `web/src/types/api.generated.ts` and `AuthorizationMatrix.cs` are 10-05, 10-06, 10-08, 10-10, 10-12, 10-14, 10-16 and 10-18, each in its own wave.
</objective>
