# Plan 10-03: The transfer rules decide every send, hand-over and refusal in the Domain

*Created: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 10 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
The staff money rules as pure Domain code at 100% coverage: the money-on-hand sum (MONEY-01), the transfer aggregate with its normal and hand-over factories (MONEY-04, D-14, D-17 to D-20, D-49, D-56), the soft delete and the remove check (D-22 to D-24, MONEY-07), and the three audit codes (AUDIT-01). The handlers in plans 10-10, 10-12 and 10-18 only read people and balances under the lock and call these rules.

Purpose: one rule set the server enforces and the browser mirrors (OR-3), with the exact UI-SPEC wording, so no handler decides a money rule on its own.
Output: `MoneyOnHand`, `MoneyRules`, `MoneyParty`, `MoneyPartyStatus`, `MoneyTransfer`, three `AuditActions` codes, and their tests.

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff land on a dashboard that shows how the business stands today: active work, money owed, money collected and money spent. Each staff member also sees the money they hold and can hand it to a coworker (owner addition, 2026-10-07)."
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
The assumption-delta scan fired (pluralization: a second way to name a transfer's sender). Until now "who sends" was always derived from the signed-in user; D-17 adds a second case where a Manager chooses a leaver as the sender.
- Primary noun: the transfer's sender, `FromUserId`, stored explicitly on every transfer.
- Decision: promote. `FromUserId` is the sender for every variant; the signed-in user is only the default source of it for a normal transfer. A hand-over is a flagged variant (`IsHandover = true`, a stored column added in plan 10-08), and `CreatedById` stays "who recorded it" (SEC-08). Rationale: a later importer (Phase 15) may write transfers whose recorder is the "Legacy import" identity, which a derived rule (recorder differs from sender means hand-over) would misread.
- Invariant test (accepted into plan 10-12): every balance reads `FromUserId` and `ToUserId` only, never `CreatedById`; a hand-over lowers the leaver's balance and leaves the recording Manager's balance unchanged.
</assumption_delta_decision>
