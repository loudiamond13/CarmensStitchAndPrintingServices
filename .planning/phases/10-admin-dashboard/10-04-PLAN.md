# Plan 10-04: The income vs expenses chart can draw a profit line, negative months and real phone widths

*Created: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 10 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
Extend the shared `IncomeExpenseChart` behind new optional props (RESEARCH Pattern 11, UI-SPEC D3): the server's profit per month as a line (D-36), the dashboard's own legend and tooltip words (D-50), below-zero income and profit (D-36), and drawing at the frame's real width on phones with every other month label when crowded (D-51). Purpose: staff read the shop's monthly profit at a glance on a phone, and the figure is the server's.
Output: the changed component, its recipe classes and tests.

</objective>
