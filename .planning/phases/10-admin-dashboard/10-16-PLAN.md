# Plan 10-16: Managers see everyone's money and transfers, and Admins see only their own

*Created: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 10 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
The OD-19 read side (MONEY-03, RESEARCH Pattern 8): everyone's balances with leavers and the total (D-42 to D-44), one person's balance and ledger (D-42), and the transfer list (D-48), each with the role check before any lookup so an Admin learns nothing about anyone else (CWE-639, CWE-204). Plans 10-19 and 10-20 draw these.

Purpose: success criterion 5's access rules: an Admin sees only their own figures and transfers and gets 403 for anyone else's; a Manager sees every staff member's balance and all transfers.
Output: the access rule, three handlers, the list request and validator, responses, reader methods, four controller actions, tests, matrix rows, contract and docs.

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff land on a dashboard that shows how the business stands today: active work, money owed, money collected and money spent. Each staff member also sees the money they hold and can hand it to a coworker (owner addition, 2026-10-07)."

</objective>
