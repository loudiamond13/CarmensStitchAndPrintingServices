# Plan 10-12: A Manager hands over money held by a former staff member, and the transfer window knows who can receive

*Created: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-05T15:30:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 10 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
The second create path (D-17, D-18) and the recipients list the transfer window needs (D-19, D-49, D-56). A Manager records a transfer from a deactivated or deleted staff member to an active one through its own endpoint, under the same `staff-money` lock (D-53), audited with who did it (AUDIT-01, D-52). Every staff member can read who may receive a transfer, with no figures for anyone.

Purpose: money held by people who left can always be handed over, and the transfer window offers exactly the people the server accepts.
Output: request, validator, handler, audit detail, recipients handler and response, reader method, controller actions, tests, matrix rows, contract and docs.

Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Staff land on a dashboard that shows how the business stands today: active work, money owed, money collected and money spent. Each staff member also sees the money they hold and can hand it to a coworker (owner addition, 2026-10-07)."

Serial contract rule: the plans that rewrite `tools/openapi-gen/openapi.v1.json`, `web/src/types/api.generated.ts` and `AuthorizationMatrix.cs` are 10-05, 10-06, 10-08, 10-10, 10-12, 10-14, 10-16 and 10-18, each in its own wave.
</objective>
