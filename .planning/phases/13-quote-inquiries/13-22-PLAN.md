<objective>
UI-SPEC R3 with D-20 to D-23: Turn into Order opens the normal New Order form prefilled from the request (13-20), the save links both and marks the request Converted, a race shows the already-converted box with a way to keep the typed order, and the order page links back to its request.

Output: the prefill flow on the order form, the R2 conversion buttons, the order page row and an `@quote` e2e.
</objective>
