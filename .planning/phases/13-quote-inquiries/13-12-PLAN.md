# Plan 13-12: Photos go with the request, and every failure keeps what the visitor typed

*Created: 2026-11-21T21:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-21T21:30:00-06:00*

<objective>
The rest of UI-SPEC Q3: the picker (13-02) joins the form, photos are uploaded one by one after the text is saved (13-05), and the visitor always sees a plain result: success, success with missing photos (D-03), rate limited (D-26), failed, or maybe sent. An expired ticket is refreshed and resent silently (Pitfall 4).

Purpose: a lead is never lost to a photo, a network drop or an old tab.
Output: the photo upload flow, the result component, the header option in the HTTP client, and e2e cases.
</objective>
