# Plan 13-08: Quote requests that never became orders, and their photos, are removed after 6 months

- INQ-07 (unclassified by the probe; flagged, not dropped): the purge counts calendar months in business time and runs daily (D-34); a request whose photos are still uploading when it turns 6 months old is impossible in practice (upload keys last 2 hours). Output: the retention constant, the job, the sweep pass, the inventory union and the three new removal reasons.
</objective>
