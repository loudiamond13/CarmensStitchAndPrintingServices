<objective>
The public photo picker (UI-SPEC Q2) as its own component, before any network code: a visitor picks, previews and removes photos, and wrong ones are refused in the browser with the server's wording (OR-3, D-01, D-02, D-05, D-06). Plan 13-12 puts it in the form and sends the photos.

Purpose: photos are the usual way teams show a logo; the picker must work on a phone with one thumb and never touch the file.
Output: QuotePhotoPicker, the quote photo rules, the helpers moved to `@/utils/images`, the Note warning icon.
</objective>
