*Created: 2026-11-21T21:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-21T21:30:00-06:00*

## Assumptions (edge probe, unresolved)

- INQ-05 (unclassified by the probe; flagged, not dropped): counts and rows are read in one request without a lock, so a pill count can differ by one from the rows while someone changes a status at the same moment; the next refresh (every 60 seconds, plan 13-15) corrects it.

Purpose: staff see every lead and how many are waiting.
Output: the Staff controller with the list and new-count actions, the reader, the validator and their tests.
</objective>
