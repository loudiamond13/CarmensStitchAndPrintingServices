# Plan 13-06: Bots are stopped by a honeypot, a minimum time and rate limits, with no CAPTCHA

*Created: 2026-11-21T21:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-21T21:30:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 13 is not in user-story form, so no story was invented.
> Spec-less probe fallback: the INQ-03 boundary, precision, idempotency and concurrency edges and the INQ-01 adjacency edge are lifted into must_haves.

<objective>
INQ-03: the create endpoint gets a server-signed form ticket (minimum time to submit), a honeypot (D-25), a per-address limit (D-26), a site-wide ceiling and a per-sender daily limit (D-37), all settings with tests and none of them a puzzle. Caught bots see a normal success; expired tickets are a 400 so a real lead is never dropped (Pitfall 4).

Purpose: keep the inbox and the alert email usable without blocking real visitors who share a mobile network.
Output: the ticket endpoint, the guards in the create handler, the rate-limit policies, the quote-requests lock and their tests.
</objective>
