## Assumptions (edge probe, unresolved)

- INQ-05 (unclassified; flagged, not dropped): adding a note does not need the row version (notes only append), but it stamps the request's UpdatedBy and UpdatedAt and moves its row version, so a status change sent with the version read before the note gets a 409 and staff load the latest.
- D-16 rating: one-way (a deleted request cannot come back). Already settled by the owner in 13-CONTEXT.md, so no new checkpoint; the rating is recorded on Task 2.

<objective>
INQ-05's notes (D-17) and the Manager's permanent delete (D-16, for example a data-removal request under RA 10173). Photos are queued for the daily removal job, never deleted inline.

Purpose: staff keep their working notes on the lead, and the shop can honour a removal request.
Output: the notes table and endpoint, the delete endpoint, two audit actions and their tests.
</objective>
