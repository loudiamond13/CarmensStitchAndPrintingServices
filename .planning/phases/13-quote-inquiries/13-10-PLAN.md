# Plan 13-10: Staff get an email for each new quote request, at most 20 an hour

*Created: 2026-11-21T21:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-21T21:30:00-06:00*

## Assumptions (for the owner, not one-way)

- RESEARCH A4: the alert is queued with the text, before the photos arrive, so "Photos: {n}" is the number of photos the visitor attached. The inbox shows which actually arrived (D-03). Waiting for the uploads would risk losing the alert when a visitor closes the tab.

<objective>
INQ-04 with D-08, D-09, D-10, D-12, D-32 and D-33: each saved request queues one summary email to the Quote alerts address (plan 13-07) in the same transaction, capped at 20 an hour with one overflow email, through the existing outbox. The test email is plan 13-13.

Purpose: staff hear about a lead fast, but the saved request and the New badge stay the safety net.
Output: two new email kinds and templates, the cap under the lock, and their tests.
</objective>
