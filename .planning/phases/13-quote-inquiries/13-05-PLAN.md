# Plan 13-05: Photos sent with a request are stored privately, and a failed photo never loses the request

*Created: 2026-11-21T21:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-21T21:30:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 13 is not in user-story form, so no story was invented.
> Spec-less probe fallback: the INQ-02 boundary and concurrency edges are lifted into must_haves.

<objective>
INQ-02 on the server: the text is saved first (13-01), then each photo travels in its own call (IIS caps a body at 11 MiB and the idempotency gate buffers in memory, RESEARCH Pattern 1, Pitfall 1). Photos go through the Phase 9 image pipeline into a private `{root}/quotes/` folder. A failed photo leaves the request saved with a missing slot (D-03).

Purpose: "a lead is never lost to a storage failure" (D-03) while every file is checked by its bytes (CWE-434, D-01).
Output: photo slots, the upload key, the photo endpoint and their tests.
</objective>
