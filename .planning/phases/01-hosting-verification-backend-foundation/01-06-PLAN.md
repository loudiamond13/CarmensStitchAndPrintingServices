<objective>

Purpose: FND-05, SEC-01, D-14, D-26, D-27. Phase 2's client and every later endpoint rely on this contract.
Output: ProblemDetails pipeline, Result/Error types and mapping, security headers middleware, web.config, dev-only OpenAPI/Scalar, and their tests.
</objective>
