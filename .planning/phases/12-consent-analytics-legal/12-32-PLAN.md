# Plan 12-32: More room in the public first download: one entry chunk, and the legal pages measured in full

*Created: 2026-11-17T03:40:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-17T03:40:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 12 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
A measured recovery for owner ruling D-42 (12-CONTEXT: free room, never raise the 150 KB limit; later entry-touching plans must fit in the room).

After wave 3, `npm --prefix web run build:budget` (the build CI runs) fails: Privacy 150.04 KB. Plan 12-09 (the consent store split into consentJar, consentRules, consentSave, consentPolicy) added about 1.4 KB to every public route and plan 12-11 (the Privacy Policy text) about 2 KB to /privacy. This plan brings every public route to at or under 148.50 KB in both builds, without raising any limit and without changing what any page shows or does, in two honest moves:

1. Package the entry's static import chain as one file (D-42). The same modules, the same order, the same routes; 7 fewer files per route and one gzip stream instead of nine. Planner measure: about 3.5 KB off every public row and about 3.6 KB off admin JS.
2. Count the legal loader chunk in the four legal rows (D-42, honest measurement). The legal routes download `legalLoaders.ts` before their page today, but the figures leave it out (about 0.55 KB). This raises the legal figures to what a visitor really downloads first.

Then record a per-route headroom table, so plans 12-13 (Cookie Settings window and footer legal row) and 12-15 (page view beacon), which both add to the public first download, know their room.

Ordering: the orchestrator runs this plan alone, after wave 3 and BEFORE the other wave 4 plans (12-13, 12-14, 12-15). None of them edits the files of this plan.

Purpose: Phase 12 ships within the PERF-01 promise with room for the rest of wave 4.
Output: one `codeSplitting` group in `web/vite.config.ts` with its config test, legal rows that count the loader in `web/scripts/budget.ts` with tests, and the figures and headroom table in `docs/web/budgets.md`.
</objective>

Why the public first download is split up today:
- Rolldown gives every set of importers its own chunk. Modules the entry imports statically that lazy chunks also import (react, the router utils, clsx, the icon registry with lucide-react, the brand parts, the button and feedback recipes) sit in 8 small shared chunks that the entry imports at once. Every route, public and admin, downloads all of them before anything renders. Each is its own gzip stream with its own import and export lines (for example consentJar: 162 B gzip for 194 B of code).
- Every public row loads 20 to 32 files: Home 31, Contact 32, Privacy 30.

Candidate C1, the `$initial` group (Task 1). `build.rolldownOptions.output.codeSplitting: { groups: [{ name: 'index', tags: ['$initial'] }] }`. Rolldown 1.2.11 documents the built-in tag `$initial` as "statically imported by a user-defined entry, or part of its static dependency chain". The group chunk takes in the entry module, so it is the entry chunk itself (file name still `assets/index-<hash>.js`, facade `index.html`); only the rolldown runtime stays a 428 B chunk.
