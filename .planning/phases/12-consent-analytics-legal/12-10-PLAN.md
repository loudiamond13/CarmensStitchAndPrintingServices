<objective>
The collect endpoint's first slice (ANL-06, ANL-05 path rule, D-21): an anonymous beacon adds one to a (business day, page key) counter for everyone who is not a bot or signed-in staff. Plan 12-14 adds the identified visit on the same endpoint.

Purpose: page views and top pages for any range come from one small anonymous table (RESEARCH Pattern 1 note), and nothing personal is stored for visitors who said no.
Output: DailyPageCounts table, the handler with its gates, the controller action, the staff marker cookie, the collect rate limit, matrix and allow-list entries.
