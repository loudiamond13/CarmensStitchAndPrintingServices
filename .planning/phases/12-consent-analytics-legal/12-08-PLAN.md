# Plan 12-08: A visitor's cookie choice is recorded as proof and saved in their browser

<objective>
The server half of consent (ANL-01, ANL-02, ANL-07): one anonymous endpoint that logs every decision, sets the necessary consent cookie, honors GPC on the server (D-20) and issues or expires the HttpOnly visitor id (ANL-05, D-41). Also the public GPC statement (D-39).

Purpose: the proof of consent is server-side and tamper-resistant, and identified analytics can never start without a logged decision (RESEARCH Anti-patterns).
Output: the ConsentRecords table and migration, the handler, the controller, the cookie helpers, rate limit, matrix and allow-list entries, OpenAPI and web types.
