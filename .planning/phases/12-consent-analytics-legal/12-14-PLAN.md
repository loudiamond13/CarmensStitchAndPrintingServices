## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-12-37 | Information disclosure | re-identification from IP (RA 10173) | high | mitigate | IpPrefix /24 and /48 first, then HMAC with a random per-day salt; salts are deleted after 2 days (plan 12-18), so old hashes cannot be recomputed. |
| T-12-38 | Information disclosure | visitor id in a database copy | medium | mitigate | Only SHA-256 of the 256-bit cookie value is stored; the cookie itself is HttpOnly. |
| T-12-39 | Spoofing | identified collection without consent | high | mitigate | Three server gates: HttpOnly visitor id (issued only with a logged yes), AllowsStats on the consent cookie, no Sec-GPC. |
| T-12-40 | Tampering | crafted speed values skewing the stats | low | mitigate | VitalBounds per metric; consented visits only; per-address rate limit. |
| T-12-41 | Denial of service | sample and visit growth | low | mitigate | 2 KB bodies, rate limit, at most three samples per report, 13-month purge (plan 12-18). |
| T-12-SC | Tampering | npm/NuGet installs | high | mitigate | No package added or bumped. |
