*Created: 2026-11-11T18:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-11T18:30:00-06:00*

<objective>
The admin tracer (UI-SPEC § S0 and § S1, D-27): the Manager-only Site Content area with its nav item and tab strip, and the Business Info tab saving through plan 11-09's `PUT /api/v1/site-settings`, with every rule checked in the browser first (OR-3), the phone repeater (D-23), the 409 banner and the dirty guard. Purpose: Managers keep the shop's facts current without a developer.
Output: nav item and icons, route, page, tab hook, Business Info tab, PhoneNumbersField, copy, schemas, service, hooks, fixtures and the e2e journey.
</objective>
