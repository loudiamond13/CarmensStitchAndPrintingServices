# Plan 11-31: Visitors open any Gallery item in a lightbox and move through it by key, tap, swipe or button

*Created: 2026-11-11T18:30:00-06:00 · Updated: 2026-11-11T18:30:00-06:00*

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 11 is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

<objective>
The Gallery lightbox (UI-SPEC § G2, App. E.3; GAL-02, C-35, D-30): a lazy dialog over the masonry from plan 11-29 with the position, wrapping Previous and Next, keys, phone tap zones and swipe, thumbnails for an order's photos, the caption and its quote button, announcements, focus return and Back-to-close. Built on the existing overlay hooks and the PhotoViewer recipe looks.

Purpose: a visitor can look closely at any piece of work on a phone or a desktop, with every way of moving through it.
Output: the lightbox, swipe and history hooks, the Gallery wiring, fixtures and e2e.
</objective>
