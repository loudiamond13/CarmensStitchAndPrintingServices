## Open question for the owner (raised at planning, 2026-11-11)

Restoring a deleted order: D-15 says deleting takes an order off the website at once, but no ruling says what a Manager's restore does.
- **A (recommended):** deleting also turns "Show on the website" off (the customer OK stays), so a restore never puts the order back by itself, the same as cancelling (D-41). Reason: nothing goes public without a deliberate step.
- **B:** a restore brings the order back on the website as it was.
