## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal for Phase 5 (verbatim): "Every staff member can manage their own name, email, password and theme. Managers can run the staff roster safely: invite, edit, lock, deactivate and delete users under guard rules. Every action is audited and can be reviewed."

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line is not in user-story form. No story was invented.

> Gap closure: closes the row-action half of 05-VERIFICATION.md gap 5 (WR-20). The gap brief asks for a 44 px tap area "without changing the visible look", but row buttons are drawn 36 px wide with only 2 px between them (App. E `.s-acts`, kept by D-31 O-8). Two side-by-side 44 px areas would overlap by 6 px, so in the overlap one button's area covers part of its neighbour's drawn box: a tap on the edge of Deactivate could trigger Delete. A full 44 x 44 area for each row button therefore needs either a small visible spacing change or an accepted 36 px width. That is a look ruling (Phase 2 D-08: the executor never decides a look-changing fix), so the owner rules first.
