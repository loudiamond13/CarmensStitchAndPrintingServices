<objective>
Persist the emailed-code challenge from plan 04-05 and give handlers the two secrets primitives they need: an HMAC that binds each code to its ticket and user, and a cryptographic random source for codes and tokens.

Purpose: AUTH-02/03 require codes stored only as HMAC-SHA256, bound to a pending-login ticket and user, single-use and compared in fixed time; AUTH-04 needs durable per-account counts that survive an app-pool recycle.
Output: the challenge tables and migration, `ITwoFactorChallenges` with its store, `ICodeMac`, `ISecureRandom`, `Auth:CodeMacKey` options, and the test key.
</objective>
