## What changed

**Task 1 (CR-01, D-45).** `signOut()` in `session.ts` returns `Promise<boolean>`: `true` only when the server ended the session (then the session ends, other tabs are told and the `signed-out` notice is set). A 409 is retried once; any other failure, or a second 409, resolves `false` and touches nothing (token, snapshot, notice, channel). `AdminShell` navigates to `/admin/login` on `true` and on `false` shows a danger toast from `ADMIN_COPY.signOutFailed` ("We couldn’t sign you out." / "Check your connection and try again.", curly apostrophe). The last failure toast id is held in a ref and dismissed on the next press, so a second failed press replaces it. The drawer stays open on failure.

**Why the 863c547 suppression flag was removed.** On a confirmed sign-out the server has already revoked the refresh family and deleted the cookie, so the sign-in route's session check can only get 401 and stays on the sign-in page with the notice. On failure the session is kept, so there is nothing to suppress. Keeping the flag would also have stopped this tab from picking up a sign-in made in another tab.

**Task 2 (WR-12, D-49).** `IPv6PrefixBytes` is 8 and the key suffix is `/64` (keys like `2001:db8:1:2::/64`). Addresses in one /64 share a bucket; sibling /64s in one /48 do not. `IpPrefix.cs` and its tests (audit /24 and /48) are untouched. The `PartitionKey` doc summary was rewritten in plain words.

**Task 3 (records).** D-45 to D-49 added to 04-CONTEXT.md with pointers from D-25 and D-39; T-04-53 amended in 04-15-PLAN.md; 04-REVIEW-FIX.md shows the final CR-01 and WR-12 state and an "Owner decisions (2026-10-15)" table (status stays `partial` because WR-06 is skipped); 04-UI-SPEC.md and CSPS-UI-SPEC.md record the Logout failure state and copy. CSPS-UI-SPEC.md gained its italic Created/Updated line and a Logout block in section 8.1.
